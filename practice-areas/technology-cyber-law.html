Practice Area

Technology Law and Cyber Law Firm

Trilect Associates advises technology companies, SaaS businesses, and enterprises on the legal issues arising from digital operations, from contracting and cybersecurity compliance to cyber fraud litigation and emerging AI regulation. Our technology and cyber law practice supports clients through both advisory and contentious matters under the Information Technology Act, 2000, the DPDP Act, 2023, and India's developing AI governance framework.

Our Core Services

Technology Contracts and Software Licensing

We draft and negotiate software licensing agreements, cloud and SaaS contracts, and IT outsourcing agreements, allocating liability, data obligations, and service levels appropriately between technology providers and their customers, whether based in India or contracting into the Indian market.

Cybersecurity and Data Breach Compliance

We advise businesses on cybersecurity obligations under the Information Technology Act, 2000, including CERT-In directions on cyber incident reporting, and support clients through data breach response, notification obligations, and remediation, working alongside our Data Protection practice on DPDP Act compliance.

Cyber Fraud, Cybercrime Litigation and IT Act Matters

We represent clients in cyber fraud and cybercrime matters, including before cyber cells and cybercrime police stations, and advise on IT Act adjudication proceedings, electronic evidence, and the prosecution and defence of cyber offences.

AI Governance and Emerging Technology Advisory

We advise businesses deploying artificial intelligence on India's developing AI governance framework, including MeitY's AI Governance Guidelines and sector specific AI regulation, alongside advisory on blockchain and cryptocurrency related legal matters.
