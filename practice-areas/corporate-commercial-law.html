Practice Area

Corporate and Commercial Law

Trilect Associates provides end to end corporate and commercial legal support across every stage of a business lifecycle, from formation and contract drafting to regulatory compliance, investment structuring, market expansion, and exit strategy. Our corporate lawyers work with founders, promoters, and in-house legal teams in India and internationally, delivering commercially practical solutions that align with business objectives while mitigating legal and regulatory risk. Whether you are a startup entering the Indian market, an established company scaling operations, or an international business structuring cross-border transactions, our corporate and commercial practice is built to support your growth at every stage.

Our Core Services

Corporate and Commercial Advisory

We advise businesses on entity formation and business structuring in India, facilitating compliance with statutory requirements under the Companies Act and related regulations, procuring licenses, registrations, and regulatory approvals, and liaising with government authorities and regulators on behalf of our clients. Our corporate advisory services support startups, growth stage companies, and multinational businesses setting up or expanding operations in India, ensuring a smooth and legally sound market entry and ongoing compliance framework.

Contract Drafting, Vetting and Negotiation

Our commercial contracts team drafts, reviews, and negotiates a wide range of agreements, including e-commerce contracts, franchise agreements, technology and SaaS agreements, master service agreements (MSA), distribution and vendor arrangements, advertising and marketing contracts, sponsorship agreements, outsourcing contracts, manufacturing agreements, and assignment, license, and novation agreements. Every contract is drafted to protect our clients' commercial interests while remaining enforceable and practical to implement, reducing the risk of future disputes.

Strategic Business Support

Beyond documentation, we act as strategic partners to our clients, assisting in structuring, reviewing, and negotiating complex business arrangements, including joint ventures, partnerships, and commercial collaborations. Our goal is to help clients achieve closure of business arrangements in a timely and commercially feasible manner, combining legal precision with business pragmatism.
