Consumer Dispute Lawyers in India

Trilect Associates represents both consumers and businesses in consumer disputes before Indian consumer forums and commissions. As e-commerce, digital payments, and online services expand rapidly across India, consumer disputes have grown correspondingly, and businesses and consumers alike need legal counsel that understands both the procedural framework of the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, and the commercial realities of the sectors involved.

Our Core Services

Consumer Complaint Advisory and Representation

We represent consumers seeking redress for deficient goods and services, and businesses defending complaints before District, State, and National Consumer Disputes Redressal Commissions, guiding clients through filing, evidence, and hearings under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.

E-Commerce and Online Consumer Protection

E-commerce continues to account for the largest share of consumer complaints in India. We advise online platforms, sellers, and consumers on compliance with the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020, covering disclosure obligations, unfair trade practices, and liability for defective products and undelivered or misrepresented services.

Sector-Focused Consumer Dispute Resolution

Alongside e-commerce, we regularly handle consumer disputes in sectors that see a disproportionate share of complaints in India, including banking, financial services and insurance, real estate and housing, travel, tourism and airlines, and consumer durables and electronics, bringing sector specific context to each matter.

Consumer Protection Compliance Advisory for Businesses

We help businesses reduce their consumer dispute exposure before it arises, advising on product liability, service deficiency risk, advertising and labelling compliance, and unfair trade practice exposure under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.
