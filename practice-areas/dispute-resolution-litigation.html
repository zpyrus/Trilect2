Practice Area

Dispute Resolution and Litigation in India

Trilect Associates' Dispute Resolution practice provides litigation, mediation, and arbitration support to clients across industries and jurisdictions in India. We handle complex and large value disputes, guiding clients through the challenges disputes can bring, while working alongside them to find solutions that are practical, commercially feasible, and time and cost effective.

Our Core Services

Advisory and Representation Across Forums

We provide legal advisory and representation before the Supreme Court of India, High Courts (including Delhi, Mumbai, Punjab and Haryana, Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Karnataka, Telangana, and Chennai), benches of the NCLT and NCLAT, Debt Recovery Tribunals (DRTs), Civil and Commercial Courts, Magistrate and Sessions Courts, Labour Courts, and ad hoc and institutional arbitral institutions, both national and international. We also appear before regulatory authorities such as the Real Estate Regulatory Authority (RERA/RERAA) and other quasi-judicial forums.

Strategic Pre-Litigation Advisory

Before a dispute reaches court, we help clients craft pre-litigation strategies to mitigate legal risk, issue and respond to legal notices, assess the merits and exposure of a potential dispute, and explore alternative dispute resolution mechanisms, including negotiation and mediation.

Arbitration Expertise

We manage complex arbitration matters, including infrastructure and construction claims, engineering contract disputes, shareholder disputes, and cross-border investment conflicts, representing clients before both domestic and international arbitral institutions.
