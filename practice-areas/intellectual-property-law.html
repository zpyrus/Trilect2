Our Core Services

Trademark, Copyright and Patent Filing

We advise clients on the registrability of trademarks, copyrights, patents, and designs, and file trademark, copyright, and patent applications in more than 50 countries, including the United States, the United Kingdom, countries across Europe, Canada, Australia, Malaysia, Thailand, Japan, and the UAE. We respond to examination reports and represent clients in hearings before the Trademarks Registry and equivalent authorities.

Opposition, Rectification and Registry Proceedings

We advise clients on opposition strategies, draft notices of opposition, counter affidavits, and rectification applications, and prepare supporting evidence, along with representation in hearings before the relevant IP registries.

IP Enforcement and Infringement Litigation

We advise and represent clients in copyright infringement suits before Indian courts, represent clients in trademark infringement suits, and provide counsel on instituting infringement and passing off actions. Our enforcement services also include DMCA takedown notices and counter statements, cease and desist notices and responses, and UDRP complaints for domain name disputes.

IP Commercial Transactions and Licensing

We advise clients on assignment of trademarks and other IP assets, and draft IP assignment agreements and commercial licensing arrangements, helping clients monetise and transfer their intellectual property while protecting their long term rights.
