Practice Area

Media and Entertainment Law Firm

Trilect Associates advises production houses, OTT platforms, artists, publishers, advertisers, and digital creators on the legal issues shaping India's fast growing media and entertainment industry. From content licensing and talent contracts to influencer marketing and media disputes, our media and entertainment practice combines commercial pragmatism with an understanding of how content is created, distributed, and monetised today.

Our Core Services

Film, Television and OTT Content Law

We advise on production, distribution, and co-production agreements for film, television, and OTT content, including content acquisition and licensing agreements for streaming platforms. Our team supports producers, studios, and platforms in structuring rights, revenue sharing, and territorial licensing across traditional and digital distribution channels.

Music, Publishing and Talent Contracts

We draft and negotiate recording contracts, music publishing agreements, and licensing deals for musicians, record labels, and publishers, along with artist, talent, and endorsement agreements that clearly define compensation, rights, and obligations for performers, celebrities, and brands.

Digital Media, Influencer and Advertising Law

We draft influencer and content creator agreements, brand collaboration and endorsement contracts, and multi-channel network (MCN) agreements, helping brands, agencies, and creators structure compliant, enforceable digital marketing relationships in a fast moving social media environment.

Media Dispute Resolution and IP Enforcement

We represent clients in media related disputes, including defamation, breach of content and licensing agreements, and copyright infringement, through litigation and arbitration, and support clients in protecting music, film, and creative IP from unauthorised use.
