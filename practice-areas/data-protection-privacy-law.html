Practice Area

Data Protection and Privacy Law (DPDP Act Compliance)

Trilect Associates' Data Protection and Privacy practice helps businesses navigate India's evolving data protection landscape, including compliance under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act). We support Indian companies, global capability centres (GCCs), and international businesses processing the personal data of individuals in India, offering strategic advisory, compliance management, policy drafting, risk assessment, and training programs tailored to each client's data processing activities and regulatory exposure.

Our Core Services

Data Privacy and DPDP Act Compliance Advisory

We provide legal opinions on cross-border data transfer and compliance with Indian data privacy and protection laws for businesses across sectors, including global capability centres (GCCs) and multinational companies with India operations. Our advisory covers applicability analysis, the obligations of data fiduciaries and data processors, and compliance strategy under the DPDP Act and its rules.

Privacy Policy and Data Agreement Drafting

We draft and update privacy policies, terms of use, disclaimers, and data retention policies to align with evolving data protection regulations. Our team also drafts memoranda of understanding, data processing agreements, and consent and data access forms, ensuring businesses meet consent and data processing requirements under applicable data protection laws.

Data Risk Assessment and ROPA Documentation

We conduct risk assessments of data processing activities, prepare Record of Processing Activities (ROPA) documentation, and provide practical risk mitigation solutions, helping businesses identify and address data protection gaps before they become regulatory or reputational liabilities.

Data Protection Training and Regulatory Advisory

We conduct interactive knowledge sessions and workshops for organisations, educating stakeholders on the applicability of data privacy and protection laws and the compliance changes they require. We assist companies in seamlessly integrating regulatory requirements into their operational frameworks, from policy design to employee training.
